What brutalist buildings teach us about honest design

ARAyesha Rahman
Nov 18, 20266 min read

Walk past a brutalist building and it tells you exactly what it is. The beams carry weight, the concrete shows its formwork, and nothing pretends to be something else.

The best interfaces, like the best buildings, show you how they work.

Digital products could learn from that honesty. Clear structure, visible states and no decoration that hides how things work.
