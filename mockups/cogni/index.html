AI summary

Machine learning lets computers learn patterns from data instead of following hand-written rules. The chapter compares supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning, and explains how models are trained, tested and improved.

  • Supervised learning trains on labelled examples to predict outcomes.
  • Unsupervised learning finds structure, such as clusters, in unlabelled data.
  • Overfitting happens when a model memorises training data and fails on new data.
